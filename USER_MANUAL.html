2. Student Guide

2.1 Registration & Login

  • Navigate to http://localhost:3000/register to register as a new campus candidate.
  • Fill in your institutional email, password, student roll number, department, degree, graduation year, and current CGPA.
  • Sign in via http://localhost:3000/login.

2.2 Resume Upload & AI Quality Scoring

Route: /student/resume

  • Upload PDF Resume: Select and upload your latest resume.
  • Resume Analysis Agent Execution: The AI extracts technical skills (Programming, Frameworks, Cloud, Databases), soft skills, work experience, projects, and certifications.
  • Resume Quality Score (0–100): View instant feedback detailing strengths and areas for improvement.
  • ChromaDB Vector Indexing: Embeddings are automatically indexed for candidate-job semantic retrieval.

2.3 Browsing Jobs & Personalized AI Match Scores

Route: /student/jobs

  • Browse open campus recruitment drives across Google, Microsoft, Amazon AWS, and partner firms.
  • Each job displays a personalized Match Score (%) computed by the Hybrid Candidate Matching Agent.
  • Inspect matched skills (green badges), missing skills (red badges), and CGPA eligibility cutoffs.
  • Click Apply Now to submit your application.

2.4 Taking Adaptive Skill Assessments

Route: /student/assessments

  • Launch tests invited by recruiters or placement officers.
  • Item Response Theory (IRT) Adaptive Engine: Questions dynamically scale in difficulty.
    • Correct Answer → Escalates from Easy → Medium → Hard.
    • Incorrect Answer → De-escalates to reinforce foundational knowledge.
  • Submit test to immediately receive your overall score, mastery level, and topic analysis.

2.5 AI Technical Mock & Screening Interviews

Route: /student/interviews

  • Launch the Interactive Conversational AI Interviewer.
  • The AI introduces itself and asks tailored role-specific questions.
  • As you respond, the agent probes deeper into edge cases, architecture, and code decisions.
  • Upon completion, the Interview Evaluation Agent scores your performance across 5 pillars (Technical Knowledge, Problem Solving, Communication, Conceptual Understanding, Role Relevance) and renders a hiring decision: HIRE / CONSIDER / REJECT.

2.6 Skill Gap Analysis & Learning Roadmap

Route: /student/skills

  • Cross-examines target job requirements against your resume, test scores, and interview performance.
  • Provides a Skill Gap Matrix with severity levels (None, Low, Medium, High, Critical).
  • Generates a Multi-Phase Learning Roadmap with recommended project ideas and timelines.

3. Administrator / Placement Officer (TPO) Guide

3.1 Real-Time Placement KPI Dashboard

Route: /admin/dashboard

Live metrics tracking total registered students, active corporate drives, student applications, completed assessments, conducted AI interviews, and placement conversion rates.

3.2 Student Management & Academic Records

Route: /admin/students

Filter candidates across departments (CS, IT, ECE, AI/ML, Data Science), degree types, and CGPAs. View resume quality scores and drive participation records.

3.3 Managing Companies & Job Description (JD) AI Ingestion

Routes: /admin/companies and /admin/jobs

  • Add corporate hiring partners and post campus jobs.
  • Paste raw JD text → The JD Analysis Agent extracts required technical skills, preferred competencies, academic eligibility cutoffs, and generates vector embeddings in ChromaDB.

3.4 Candidate Ranking & Human-in-the-Loop Overrides

Route: /admin/candidates

  • Applicants are ranked by the Hybrid Match Score.
  • Inspect the explainability modal showing exact sub-scores and textual citations.
  • Administrative Override: Modify applicant status to SHORTLISTED or OFFERED with administrative notes.

3.5 Assessment Creation & AI Question Generation

Route: /admin/assessments

Generate authenticated 4-option MCQs using the Question Generation Agent, complete with explanations, skill tagging, and difficulty levels.

3.6 Campus Analytics & Pipeline Visualizations

Route: /admin/analytics

Interactive charts for department placement distributions, assessment score histograms, skill demand vs. campus supply, and recruitment funnel stages.

3.7 Audit Logs & AI Agent Run Telemetry

Routes: /admin/audit-logs and /admin/agent-logs

  • PostgreSQL Audit Logs: Immutable records of all user actions, logins, status changes, and test submissions.
  • MongoDB Agent Runs: Comprehensive telemetry tracking execution times, models, retrieval sources, and output snapshots for all 8 agents.

4. Corporate Recruiter Guide

Routes: /recruiter/dashboard, /recruiter/jobs, /recruiter/candidates

  • Post company job requirements and define CGPA criteria.
  • View candidate pools ranked by the Hybrid Matching Agent.
  • Inspect verified resumes, adaptive test accuracy, and 5-pillar AI interview evaluation reports to issue campus offers.

5. AI Architecture & Hybrid Scoring Formula

Hybrid Scoring Formula
Final Score = 0.30 * Semantic + 0.40 * Skill Overlap + 0.20 * Eligibility + 0.10 * Experience
Pillar Weight Evaluation Mechanism
Semantic Match 30% Dense cosine similarity between student and job vector embeddings in ChromaDB.
Skill Overlap 40% Hard token matching between student competencies and mandatory technical requirements.
Eligibility 20% Boolean verification of student CGPA against minimum job cutoff.
Experience 10% Evaluation of internship duration, project complexity, and portfolio relevance.

6. Responsible AI & Transparency

  • Zero Demographic Bias: Protected characteristics (gender, race, religion, caste, disability) are strictly excluded from ranking models.
  • Explainable Recommendations: Every match provides citations and clear reasoning.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Authority: AI acts as decision support; final authority rests with placement officers and recruiters.
